## About me

I build robots that can work safely alongside people. My research experience is at the **Human-Centered Robotics Lab (HCR-L), UET Lahore**, mainly on the **Franka Emika Panda** with **ROS 2**, covering manipulation, impedance control and perception. I'm in my final year (expected July 2027, CGPA 3.717/4.00) and applying to master's programs in robotics.

🌐 **See my full portfolio, projects and CV at [fidahussain.me](https://fidahussain.me)**

## Currently working on

- 🤖 **CASCADE**, my final year project: a proactive human-robot collaboration system that predicts human actions during shared assembly and executes collision-aware, anticipatory tasks on the Panda. [Read more →](https://fidahussain.me/cascade.html)
- 📊 Building a custom RGB-D hand-tracking dataset for human intention prediction (LSTM / Transformer models).
